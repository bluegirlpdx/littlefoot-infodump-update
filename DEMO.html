🎨 How It Looks in Context

The Byzantine Empire,5 also referred to as the Eastern Roman Empire, was the continuation of the Roman Empire centered in Constantinople during Late Antiquity and the Middle Ages.

The city of Constantinople6 was founded by Constantine the Great in 330 AD and became the capital of the Eastern Roman Empire. It remained the capital for over a thousand years, until it fell to the Ottoman Turks in 1453.7

The Justinian Code,8 compiled under Emperor Justinian I, was one of the most important legal achievements of the Byzantine Empire and influenced European law for centuries.
